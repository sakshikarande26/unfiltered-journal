# the-unfiltered-journal

A weekly photo journal that turns your goals into proof.

the-unfiltered-journal is a local-first journal you post to once a week. Five
photos, a few honest words, up to five private goals. No edits, no deletes, no
algorithm.

## What it does

- One post per week, locked forever when the week ends. The gap from a missed
  week stays, because the record is honest.
- Up to 5 photos per entry, shown as a stack, plus one thing you liked and one
  thing you found challenging.
- Up to 5 private goals per post, frozen at publish. Complete them whenever,
  even years later.
- Goal text is never shared. Only the completed count (3/5) can be disclosed,
  per entry, at your choice.
- A private year grid of all your weeks: filled, missed, and still ahead of
  you.
- Local-first: your archive is a folder of files you own. SQLite is just a
  rebuildable index.
